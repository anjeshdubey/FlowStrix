# Shipping FAQ

## Delivery Timeframes

| Shipping Method | Delivery Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5-7 business days | Free (orders over $50) |
| Express | 2-3 business days | $9.99 |
| Next Day | 1 business day | $19.99 |
| Same Day (metro areas) | Same day if ordered before 12pm | $29.99 |

## Tracking

- Tracking number emailed within 24 hours of shipping
- Real-time tracking available at: orders.example.com/track
- Carriers: UPS, FedEx, USPS (varies by location and item size)

## Common Questions

**Q: My tracking hasn't updated in 3+ days.**
A: This can happen during carrier transfers. If no update after 5 business days from ship date, contact us for investigation.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
A: Yes, if the order hasn't shipped yet. Contact us immediately.

**Q: What if I'm not home for delivery?**
A: Carrier will attempt delivery up to 3 times, then hold at local facility for 7 days.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
A: Currently US and Canada only. International expansion planned for Q3 2024.

## Lost/Damaged in Transit

- If marked "delivered" but not received: File a claim within 48 hours
- If package arrived damaged: Take photos, contact us within 7 days
- Replacement or refund at customer's choice for transit damage
