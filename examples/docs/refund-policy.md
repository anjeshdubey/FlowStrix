# Refund Policy

## Eligibility Criteria

A refund is eligible when ALL of the following conditions are met:

1. **Time Window:** Purchase was made within the last 30 calendar days
2. **Order Status:** Order must have a status of "delivered" or "shipped"
3. **Not Previously Refunded:** The order has not already been refunded or credited
4. **Item Condition:** Applies to damaged, defective, or incorrect items (no condition requirement for returns within 7 days)

## Refund Amounts

- **Full refund:** Item is damaged, defective, or wrong item shipped
- **Full refund (no questions):** Return requested within 7 days of delivery
- **Partial refund (80%):** Return requested 8-30 days after delivery for non-defective items
- **No refund:** Purchase older than 30 days

## Approval Thresholds

| Refund Amount | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| $0 - $500 | Auto-approved by agent |
| $500 - $2,000 | Senior support agent approval |
| $2,000+ | Manager approval required |

## Exceptions

- **Subscription products:** Prorated refund based on usage
- **Digital downloads:** No refund after download/activation
- **Gift cards:** Non-refundable
- **Sale items:** Final sale items marked "non-returnable" are not eligible

## Processing Timeline

- Refund initiated: Immediate
- Refund to original payment method: 3-5 business days
- Refund to store credit: Instant

## Customer Communication Guidelines

- Always verify eligibility before promising a refund
- If ineligible, offer alternatives: store credit, exchange, partial credit
- For high-value refunds requiring approval, set expectation of 24-hour response
- Never disclose internal approval thresholds to customers
